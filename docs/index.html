Why local

Self-driven means self-hosted.

Renting intelligence is fine until the terms change. Running it yourself is the difference between using AI and owning it.

Private by construction

Your documents, your voice, your images — processed on your CPU or GPU. Privacy isn't a policy you trust; it's a wire that isn't there.

No meter running

Open weights and open runtimes. Once the model is on disk the cost of a thousand prompts is the electricity, not an invoice.

Sovereign compute

An agent is only as self-sovereign as the machine it runs on. Local inference is the compute layer under a KERI-anchored identity: yours, end to end.

Where to start

Three paths in.

You don't need all ten on day one. Pick the path that matches the machine on your desk.

PATH A · 10 MINUTES

Just chat, privately

Any laptop. No terminal.

  1. 1Install GPT4All or Jan
  2. 2Download a small model from inside the app
  3. 3Point it at a folder of your own documents
PATH B · AN AFTERNOON

Build on a local API

For anyone who writes code or automations.

  1. 1Install Ollama, pull a model
  2. 2Add LocalAI if you need OpenAI-shaped endpoints
  3. 3Add whisper.cpp for voice, ComfyUI for images
PATH C · A WEEKEND

Own the whole engine

Apple Silicon or a GPU box. You want control.

  1. 1Build llama.cpp from source, or mlx-lm on a Mac
  2. 2Front it with text-generation-webui
  3. 3Cluster spare machines with exo to run the big ones
Local + identity

The model runs on your machine. The agent answers to you.

Local inference solves where the compute happens. It doesn't, on its own, solve who the agent is or what it's allowed to do. selfdriven pairs the two.

# a local agent, delegated under your AID agent selfdriven-local-01 model qwen3:8b ← served by Ollama, localhost:11434 aid EKE4g_0hDGBOqDLKzNBT3kFOPxoP7wXkqt event dip ← delegated inception, scoped & time-limited scope read:documents write:drafts expires 2026-09-19T00:00:00+10:00

Your identity, not a platform account

The agent operates under a KERI AID delegated from yours — a dip event, witnessed and pre-rotated. Revoke it and the agent is gone.

Scoped authority

An ACDC credential says exactly what the agent may touch. Local compute plus explicit scope is what makes an agent yours rather than merely on your laptop.

Verifiable output

Every action the agent takes is signed under its delegated keys and anchored in a KEL. Private in, provable out.
